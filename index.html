AhoiSports

AI-native platform for professional sports organizations

Built around three pillars — and beneath them, a system designed to act, not just report. AI agents detect trends across live data and trigger the right workflows, model-routing sends each task to the model best suited to it, and a data platform keeps every club in full ownership of its data.

1Fan Engagement — deepening the relationship between clubs and their supporters.
2Commercial Operations — turning data into sharper decisions and everyday efficiency.
3Sport Performance — intelligence for the people on the pitch.
