AhoiSports

AI-native platform for professional sports organizations

Built around three pillars — and beneath them, a system designed to act & enhance, not just report. AI detects trends across data and triggers the right workflows, while keeping every club in ownership of its data.

1Fan Engagement — reaching every supporter personally across app, website, streaming and email, not mass communication.
2Commercial Operations — ticketing, merchandising and sponsorships, optimized through data to unlock revenue.
3Sport Performance — player analysis, scouting and squad planning, all data-driven.

The interesting part of applied AI isn't the demo — it's the plumbing underneath: agents, model-routing, and a data layer each club fully owns. AI that disappears into the product and simply makes it better.
